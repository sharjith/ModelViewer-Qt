Lesson 25: Plots and Results Played Together

Introduction

Lessons 19 to 24 gave you simulation results and 3D plots separately. They live in the same scene, and when both change over time one playback bar can drive them together. This lesson traces particles through a time-dependent flow as pathlines, animates them, and plays them in step with a result.

Pathlines from a CSV Time Series

A pathline is the track of a particle carried by a flow that changes with time. Give ModelViewer the velocity field on a regular grid, one table of rows for every time, and it releases particles and integrates their paths through the changing field.

Step 1: Choose the source
In the Add 3D Plot dialog set Data source to CSV time series (pathlines) and open pathlines_double_gyre.csv: Shadden's double gyre, two counter-rotating cells whose dividing line oscillates, sampled at 21 times.
Step 2: Check the columns
Time column, X, Y, an optional Z, and U, V, W are guessed from the header names (t or time, x, u ...) and can be changed. The status line reports either that the rows form a complete grid and how many seeds and segments result, or why the table cannot be used.
Step 3: Build
Click Preview, then Build Plot. The trails are lines coloured by time, with a legend that runs from the start to the end time. The seeds are released along Y at the middle of the X range.
Screenshot: tutorial_25_double_gyre_pathlines.png (700x450)
Pathlines of the double gyre, coloured by time
pathlines_rising_vortex_3d.csv is a 3-D version with a Z column: a rotation whose rate pulses in time plus an oscillating vertical velocity, so the trails climb as they circle. Delete a line from a copy of a table and the status line says it is not a complete grid and how many rows it needs.

Pathlines from a Formula

Formula pathlines (time-dependent) needs no file: u(x,y,z,t), v(x,y,z,t) and w(x,y,z,t) are expressions in position and time, with seeds set by Y range / samples and the time interval by T range / steps. Presets: Pulsating Vortex, Double Gyre, Travelling Wave and Oscillating Updraft.

Here t is time. In Formula streamlines the field is steady, and in Parametric curve t is only the curve parameter - do not mix them up.

The Pulsating Vortex preset: rings whose colour bands bunch up where the particle slows

Animating Pathlines

Step 1: Select the plot
Choose the pathline plot in Active plot in the 3D Plot tab.
Step 2: Tick Animate pathlines
Tick Animate pathlines (timeline). A timeline appears at the top of the viewport with the controls you know from Lesson 19. The whole plot shows until you press play; from the end, play starts over.
Step 3: Press play
The trails are drawn only up to the current time, with a dot at the head of each, coloured by the current time. Drag the slider back and forth, switch Loop off, or try the speed choices from 0.5x to 4x.
Screenshot: tutorial_25_animated_pathlines.png (700x420)
Pathlines part-way through the animation, with their head dots and the timeline
The animation is a viewing aid: it is not saved with the file, and the dots are not exported or ray traced. Hiding the plot in the Scene Tree removes the timeline and pauses playback.

One Playback Bar for Both

The timeline plays whichever item is chosen in the box at its left. The box lists every multi-step simulation result (Simulation: name) and the animated pathline plot (Pathlines: name), and, when both can play, All together. With only one playable item the box is hidden. The legends of a result and a plot stack in one column, each headed with its owner's name.

Step 1: Open the cavity result
Open sample-models/Simulation/openfoam_cavity/cavity.foam and choose the velocity magnitude (U, Magnitude) in the Simulation Results tab.
Step 2: Add the matching pathlines
In the 3D Plot tab choose Add 3D Plot..., source CSV time series (pathlines), open pathlines_openfoam_cavity.csv from sample-models/Plot3D and map Time = t, X = x, Y = y, Z = z, U = u, V = v, W = w. That file holds the cell-centre velocity of the same case on a plane just above its top face, so both share coordinates and real time. Build, and hide the plot's axes box if it clutters the view.
Step 3: Play both
Tick Animate pathlines (timeline), choose All together in the playback box and press play. The trails grow while the field steps from 0 to 2 s; the bar reads the current time and step, for example t = 1 - step 3 of 5. Both follow real time: the result holds each step until the next one's time.
Screenshot: tutorial_25_cavity_with_pathlines.png (700x450)
The cavity result and its pathlines playing together, with the playback box showing All together
A modal result such as FEM_box_modes.frd, whose steps are frequencies in hertz, cannot share a time axis with pathlines: All together then plays them by progress instead of by time.
