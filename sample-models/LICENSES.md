# Sample models: sources and licences

Every file under `sample-models/` is listed here with where it comes from. The project itself is GPL-3.0; the samples below are either the author's own work or
come from sources whose licence allows redistribution with the notices given. Anything of unknown origin is not shipped.

## The author's own work (GPL-3.0, like the rest of the project)

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `Skate-Board-Assy/` | A skateboard assembly modelled by the author (STEP, IGES, OBJ, glTF and a saved session) |
| `Tea-Pot/` | The teapot used throughout the tutorial |
| `Cube/`, `Cube.*`, `Sphere*.*`, `SplitSphere.*`, `SphereJoined.*`, `Hemisphere.*`, `Cylinder.*`, `OpenCylinder.*`, `OpenCone.*`, `ClosedCone.*`, `TorusTest*.obj`, `SeamMarkTest.*`, `RepairMeshTest.*`, `TwoTrianglesSharingVertex.*`, `ThreeTrianglesSharingVertex.*`, `BezierSaddlePatch.obj` | Small test meshes made for the repair, UV, subdivision and analysis tools |

| `Plot3D/` | Plot data and images generated by the project's scripts (see its `README.txt`) |
| `Simulation/` (except `real/`, the MED files and `beampl.frd`, below) | Result files produced by CalculiX / scripts for these samples, and the generator scripts (see its `README.txt`) |

## Generated with Open CASCADE Technology's sample scripts

`ANC101.*`, `CAD.*`, `bottle.*`, `cutter.*`, `drill.*` and `MBB Gehause Rohteil.*` (STEP, BREP and OBJ) were produced by running the sample scripts in
`resources/samples/tcl/` of Open CASCADE Technology (`ANC101.tcl`, `cad.tcl`, `bottle.tcl`, `cutter.tcl`, `drill.tcl`, `MBBGehauseRohteil.tcl`).
Open CASCADE Technology is (c) Open CASCADE SAS, licensed under the GNU LGPL 2.1 with the Open CASCADE exception 1.0; these files are output data made with it.
ANC101 and Gehause Rohteil are reconstructions of the CAM-I test parts used to compare modelling systems in 1979 and 1983.
Open CASCADE Technology: https://dev.opencascade.org

## Not part of the project

Models that were here before and could not be redistributed (a CC BY-NC shuttle, three engine and grader archives, a vendor actuator, the
