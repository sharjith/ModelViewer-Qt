# Standalone XT implementation and distribution assessment

15 November 2026, branch `feature/parasolid-importer`.

## Decision supported by the evidence

A bounded, independently authored C++ reader for lawfully obtained standalone X_T files is a defensible interoperability project. The evidence supports proceeding with original parser and geometry code without integrating the Siemens kernel. It does not establish freedom to distribute every possible implementation. The release questions are the provenance and redistribution rights of the base layouts and fixtures, applicable agreements, and patent claims covering the actual implementation in the intended markets.

Siemens describes XT as a published interchange format, and the April 2008 reference expressly addresses independent translators. Its notice reserves rights in the publication. The July 2022 reference carries a proprietary-document notice. Neither inspected standalone reference contains an express redistribution or patent grant comparable to the JT terms. Plain text is a transport property. [Siemens XT product page](https://www.siemens.com/en-us/products/plm-components/parasolid/data-access-translation/) · [April 2008 reference, mirrored copy](https://ww3.cad.de/foren/ubb/uploads/schulze/XT_Format_April_2008_tcm73-62642.pdf)

## Corrections to the supplied guide and conversation

- **Copyright and independent implementation.** U.S. §102(b) excludes functional ideas, systems and methods from copyright protection. *Google v. Oracle* assumed copyrightability for argument and found fair use on its facts. *Sega v. Accolade* addressed necessary intermediate copying on its facts. Neither establishes a mandatory clean-room procedure or blanket immunity. In the EU, *SAS Institute v. World Programming*, paragraphs 39–46 and 60–67, distinguishes functional data formats from protected program expression and original expression in manuals. Paragraph 45 leaves open distinct protection under general copyright law for an original work. Code may need the same field order, numeric tags and widths to interoperate. Original code and the minimum necessary wire facts are a stronger basis than a comprehensive table transcription, including one mechanically converted from the manual. [17 U.S.C. §102](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-118/t17/s102/) · [Google v. Oracle](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/20pdf/18-956_d18f.pdf) · [Sega v. Accolade](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F2/977/1510/305345/) · [CJEU judgment](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=celex%3A62010CJ0406)
- **Contracts and access.** The agreements that apply depend on who obtained the files, how, and under what terms. India §52(1)(ab) concerns necessary interoperability information unavailable elsewhere and a lawful possessor. EU Directive 2009/24/EC distinguishes observation in Article 5(3) from limited decompilation in Article 6. Article 8 voids contractual provisions contrary to specified exceptions. U.S. §1201(f) is a conditional circumvention exception. U.S. trade-secret law distinguishes independent derivation from improper acquisition and breaches of secrecy duties. Parsing authorized, unprotected exports avoids relying on those exceptions to inspect a proprietary binary or extract its catalogs. Public download access alone establishes neither sample redistribution rights nor the provenance of a documentation mirror. [India Copyright Act](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/15356/1/the_copyright_act%2C_1957.pdf) · [EU Software Directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?qid=1781549604100&uri=CELEX%3A32009L0024) · [17 U.S.C. §1201](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-118/t17/s1201/) · [18 U.S.C. §1839](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-138/t18/s1839/)
- **Patents and GPLv3.** The claim that interoperability layouts are categorically patent-free is unsupported. Independent invention and use of an open-source license do not establish freedom to operate. Patent scope is claim- and jurisdiction-specific. Review the particular algorithms and intended territories. GPLv3 §§11–12 matter if a permission comes with obligations affecting recipients. Open-source reader licenses also require checking the provenance and terms of individual code, data and dependencies. [35 U.S.C. §271](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-120/t35/s271/) · [GPLv3](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html)
- **Embedded XT in JT.** Siemens JT 10.6 Rev D, printed p. ii, gives conditional copyright permission for JT reader software and necessary use of the listed structures. The separate patent notice is limited to named rights required for specification-compliant JT. Keep any JT-only material within that scope. ISO publication supplies a technical standard, with patent permissions handled separately. Annex F.3, printed p. 256, specifies **neutral binary** for JT XT B-Rep. A text reader cannot consume it after decompression. Share normalized records and geometry after separate transport readers. Qualify JT PMI identifiers and units from the applicable JT elements and controls. [Siemens JT reference download](https://www.siemens.com/en-gb/products/plm-components/jt/jt-open-program/) · [Siemens JT patent notice](https://www.plm.automation.siemens.com/media/global/en/Siemens-PLM-JT-Data-Format-Reference-Patent-Clarification-Notice-mi-X1_tcm27-58013.pdf) · [ISO patent policy](https://www.iso.org/iso-standards-and-patents.html)

## Application to the current branch

The current layout loader and research decoder accept a separately supplied base table. The probe used manually entered reference layouts. A local-only table avoids bundling it with the viewer. Any proposal to distribute the table, automate its reproduction from the manual, or rely on it as the shipped solution still needs review of the underlying rights and terms. Embedded edits depend on copied or unchanged base fields. They do not eliminate the base layout dependency.

Develop the generic text reader and bounded analytic geometry mapping in original code. Use a minimal layout for the supported types and document where each required field order, type and tag came from. Use lawful, self-authored exports with known dimensions for validation and record rights before redistributing any output. Keep samples, reference PDFs and the local probe table out of Git. Describe the approach as independent implementation from documentation and observed files. The existing probe provenance does not establish a formally isolated clean room.

Before public distribution, have counsel review the actual proposed layout artifacts, their source history, any operative terms and patent exposure in relevant markets. Written Siemens clarification could address whether a minimal standalone reader layout may be shipped under GPLv3 and whether Siemens offers applicable patent permissions. A Siemens permission would cover the rights within its terms. This assessment did not examine signed agreements, verify the origin of the July 2022 copy, conduct a patent claim search or review, or audit the branch file by file.
